Your files contain important data. UMiX is engineered to process your files right in your browser memory.

Conversions run in client-side JavaScript. Files are processed locally and never uploaded to remote servers.

Instant Processing

No waiting in upload queues or download links. Calculations and file tasks execute instantly on your device.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to any server?

No. All conversions run locally inside your web browser using modern WebAssembly and JavaScript libraries (PDF.js, PDF-lib, SheetJS, JSZip). Your files are never transmitted to our servers.

Does UMiX preserve every document feature?

Most formatting, text, and structure are preserved. Complex proprietary fonts, specialized macros, or encrypted files may require manual review.

What formats are currently supported?

UMiX supports PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), and TXT files, plus specialized utilities for Base64, URLs, and JSON.

Is UMiX Converter free to use?

Yes, all conversion tools are free to use without requiring an account or subscription.
